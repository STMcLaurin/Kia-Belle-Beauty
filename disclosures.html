Disclosures & Policies

How Kia Belle Beauty communicates about affiliates, AI-generated visuals, and shopping.

Affiliate disclosure

Some future links may be affiliate links. If you purchase through a qualifying link, Kia Belle Beauty may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We will identify qualifying promotional links and disclose material relationships clearly.

AI-generated imagery

Some videos and images feature AI-assisted creative concepts. Such visuals are illustrative and may not accurately reproduce every detail of actual products. Product descriptions, prices, availability, claims and links should be checked against the participating retailer.

Purchasing and checkout

Affiliate purchases occur on the merchant's website under that merchant's policies. Kia Belle Beauty does not currently offer direct checkout or claim to hold inventory. Direct sales will be introduced only after payment, fulfillment, and consumer policies are established.

Privacy and contact

This static website does not provide account creation, a customer database, or a connected inquiry submission service. When you use the inquiry form it prepares text locally for you to copy; it is not transmitted to us. The embedded videos and externally hosted fonts may be requested from their respective providers. A complete privacy policy will be finalized before analytics, tracking, forms or payments go live.

Intellectual property

The example commercials are independent advertising concepts, not evidence of endorsement by the product brands. Third-party product names belong to their respective owners.

These are launch-stage disclosures, not a substitute for legal review of your eventual commerce and privacy policies.
